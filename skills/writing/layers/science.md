# Scientific writing (voice)

The citation and verification rules in `references/scientific.md` still apply. This layer covers how the text sounds.

- Open with what the paper or section is about, in terms a first-time reader can follow, before any method or number.
- Significance tacked on reads as machine-made: "-ing" tails ("highlighting its importance"), grand stakes, a closing line that restates the paragraph, "Despite these challenges..." endings. End on the last result.
- Keep one term per concept for the whole text. A new synonym in every sentence tells the reader there is a new concept.
- Uniform rhythm is a tell: every sentence 15 to 25 words, every paragraph three or four sentences, nothing but commas and periods. Let sentence and paragraph length follow the content.
- State a limitation as a plain fact about the study, clearly and once.
- Rule-of-three lists by reflex and the same opening on consecutive sentences give the text away. List as many items as there are.
- No headings or bullets inside a short section, and no bold-first bullets.
